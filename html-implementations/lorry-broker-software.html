Sound Familiar?

These are the daily problems Indian lorry brokers face without the right software.

📱 Bookings lost in WhatsApp

Shipper orders arrive via WhatsApp, phone, and in person — with no central record. A missed message = a missed trip = a lost client.

📒 Commission disputes

Broker commission calculated on paper is always disputed. "You said 2%, not 2.5%." Without digital records, every settlement is an argument.

🧾 Bilty generation is slow

Hand-writing bilty takes 15 minutes. Mistakes require re-writing. Lost carbon copies cause legal trouble. Digital bilty takes 2 minutes and never gets lost.

⚠️ E-way bill penalties

Manually generating e-way bills on the government portal is error-prone and time-consuming. Missed e-way bills attract heavy GST penalties.

How Cargo Simplify Solves Each Problem

Booking Management

All Cargo Bookings in One Place

Accept bookings from shippers by phone, WhatsApp, or web form. Every booking is timestamped, assigned to a lorry and driver, and tracked from pickup to delivery.

  • Central booking register — no more WhatsApp hunting
  • Auto-assign lorry based on route and availability
  • Send booking confirmation to shipper instantly
  • Track booking status: pending → in-transit → delivered

Bilty / LR Generation

GST-Compliant Bilty in 2 Minutes

Generate a complete, print-ready Lorry Receipt (bilty/LR) the moment a booking is confirmed. Auto-numbered series, all GST-required fields pre-filled, and sharable via WhatsApp.

  • Auto-populate consignor, consignee, and freight details from booking
  • GST-compliant format with all required fields
  • Sequential LR number management — no duplicates
  • Print or share as PDF via WhatsApp or email
  • Linked to e-way bill for compliance
Note: A bilty issued through Cargo Simplify includes all fields required under Indian transport law and GST regulations, reducing legal disputes.

Commission Tracking

Automated Broker Commission — Zero Disputes

Set commission rates per shipper, per route, or per contract. Cargo Simplify automatically calculates broker commissions on every trip and maintains a complete ledger.

  • Commission auto-calculated on booking confirmation
  • Separate ledgers for shipper receivables and fleet owner payables
  • Monthly/quarterly commission summary reports
  • Pending payment alerts and aging reports
  • Export to Excel or transfer to Tally

E-Way Bill

E-Way Bill in One Click

Generate e-way bills directly from your Cargo Simplify booking — no separate login, no copy-pasting. The system pulls all required details from the booking and submits to the GST portal.

  • One-click e-way bill generation from any booking
  • Direct API submission to GST e-way bill portal
  • Auto-extension alerts before 24-hour expiry
  • Bulk e-way bill generation for multiple trips
  • E-way bill number saved against bilty automatically

Tally Integration

Transfer to Tally — No Double Entry

All bookings, freight income, broker commissions, and expenses automatically sync to Tally Prime or TallyERP 9. Your accountant gets clean, GST-ready ledgers without manual data entry.

  • Works with Tally Prime and TallyERP 9
  • One-click transfer of all transport transactions
  • GST purchase/sale ledgers auto-populated
  • Vehicle-wise and party-wise accounts
  • No manual journal entries needed
