Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free trial available? +
Yes — we offer a free demo session where our team walks you through the full platform. Contact us to schedule your demo. We can also set up a trial account for you to test with your own data.
Can I migrate data from my current software or Excel? +
Yes. Our team assists with data migration from Excel, other transport software, and paper records. We support import of lorry master data, party ledgers, and historical trip records.
Does Cargo Simplify work with Tally Prime and TallyERP 9? +
Yes. Cargo Simplify integrates with both Tally Prime and TallyERP 9. All transport transactions, freight income, commissions, and party ledgers sync directly to your Tally company file.
What happens to my data if I stop the subscription? +
You retain full access to export all your data before the subscription ends. We provide your complete data in Excel/CSV format on request. We never hold your data hostage.
Is GST invoice provided? +
Yes. A proper GST invoice is provided for all purchases. Our GSTIN is available on the invoice for your input tax credit.
How many lorries / trips can I manage? +
There is no limit on the number of lorries, trips, bookings, or bilty/LR numbers. All plans include unlimited data entry.
Is technical support available in Hindi? +
Yes. Our support team provides assistance in English, Hindi, and Gujarati. Phone support is available during business hours (Mon–Sat, 9 AM–6 PM IST).
