Everything You Need in One Platform

Cargo Simplify replaces disconnected tools with a single, integrated transport management system.

📄 Bilty / LR Generation

Generate GST-compliant Lorry Receipts (bilty) instantly. Auto-numbered series, print-ready format, sharable via WhatsApp or email.

📅 Transport Booking

Accept bookings from shippers, assign lorries and drivers, track trip status in real time, and update consignees automatically.

💰 Broker Commission Tracking

Automatically calculate broker commissions per trip. Track receivables from shippers and payables to fleet owners in one dashboard.

🧾 Tally Integration

Transfer all transport transactions to Tally Prime or TallyERP 9 with one click. No duplicate data entry. GST ledgers auto-populated.

🗺️ E-Way Bill Compliance

Generate e-way bills directly from bookings. One-click upload to the GST portal. Avoid penalties for missing e-way bill compliance.

📱 Mobile App (Android & iOS)

Drivers and field staff can view trip details, update delivery status, and capture proof-of-delivery from their smartphones.
