About Cargo Simplify

We build transport management software specifically for Indian lorry brokers, fleet owners, and cargo handlers — the people who keep India's goods moving.

Our Story

Cargo Simplify was born from a simple observation: Indian lorry brokers and fleet owners were managing complex, high-value operations using paper registers, WhatsApp groups, and handwritten bilty books.

A missed e-way bill meant a ₹10,000 penalty. A lost bilty copy triggered a legal dispute. Commission calculations done on paper ended in arguments. There had to be a better way.

We built Cargo Simplify to digitize exactly what Indian transporters do every day — from receiving a cargo booking to generating a bilty, assigning a lorry, tracking the trip, calculating commission, and posting the final entry to Tally.

We're based in Vapi, Gujarat — and we build software that speaks the language of Indian transport.

What We Build

🖥️ Desktop Application

A full-featured Windows application that works offline. Ideal for lorry brokers with a single-office setup who prefer speed over cloud dependency.

🌐 Web Platform (GTA FUSION)

Cloud-based transport management accessible from any browser. Multi-user, multi-branch, real-time. Log in at app.cargosimplify.com.

📱 Mobile App

Android and iOS apps for drivers and field staff to view trip details, update delivery status, and capture proof-of-delivery from anywhere in India.

Who We Serve

🏢 Lorry Brokers / Commission Agents

Transport commission agents who connect shippers with fleet owners. We help manage bookings, bilty, commission ledgers, and party settlements digitally.

🚛 Fleet Owners

Own 5 to 500 trucks? Cargo Simplify tracks your vehicles, manages trip assignments, calculates trip profitability, and integrates with Tally for accounting.

📦 Warehouse Managers

Coordinate inbound and outbound shipments, track inventory movement, manage dispatch records, and stay connected with your transport team in real time.

📋 Cargo Handlers

Field teams who load, unload, and deliver cargo. The mobile app gives them the information they need and lets them update status without calling the office.
