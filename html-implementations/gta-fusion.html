What is GTA FUSION?

GTA FUSION is the brand name for Cargo Simplify's cloud-based transport management platform. When you sign up for Cargo Simplify's Web or Enterprise plan, you access the system through the GTA FUSION interface at app.cargosimplify.com.

The name "GTA" stands for Goods Transport Agency — the GST designation for transport service providers in India. FUSION reflects the integration of all transport operations: booking, bilty, fleet, accounts, and compliance in one platform.

  • Accessible from any browser — no installation required
  • Android and iOS mobile apps available
  • Multi-user, multi-branch access
  • Real-time trip tracking and status updates
  • All Cargo Simplify features in one interface

GTA FUSION Features

📅 Booking Management

Create, assign, and track cargo bookings. Multi-party support for shipper → broker → carrier workflows.

📄 Bilty / LR Generation

Digital, GST-compliant bilty generation with auto-numbering and WhatsApp/email sharing.

🗺️ E-Way Bill

One-click e-way bill generation and submission via GST portal API integration.

💰 Commission Tracking

Automated broker commission calculation, ledgers, and party-wise settlement reports.

🧾 Tally Integration

Sync all transport transactions to Tally Prime or TallyERP 9 with one click.

📱 Mobile App

Drivers and field staff use the Android/iOS app to update trip status and capture POD.
