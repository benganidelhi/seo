The 5 Problems with Excel for Transport Management

1. Excel has no bilty / LR generation

Excel can store data, but it cannot generate a proper bilty (Lorry Receipt). You end up maintaining a separate pre-printed bilty book, then manually transferring data from Excel to the bilty. That's double work — and double the chance of a mistake.

2. No e-way bill integration

For every consignment above ₹50,000, an e-way bill is legally required. With Excel, e-way bills are generated separately on the government portal by copying data from your spreadsheet. One missed e-way bill = minimum ₹10,000 GST penalty.

3. Commission calculations break down

Broker commission in Excel is handled by formulas. Once rates vary per client, per route, or per contract — the formulas multiply in complexity. Errors are common. Disputes are worse.

4. No multi-user access

Excel files are either on one computer or shared via Google Sheets/email. Drivers can't update trip status. Your accountant works on a different copy. Field staff have no visibility. Everyone is always working on stale data.

5. Tally entry is manual double-work

After every booking, someone has to manually enter the same data into Tally. This is duplicate work that introduces errors and delays your month-end closing by days.

3 Real Scenarios Where Software Wins Over Excel

Scenario 1: The ₹10,000 E-Way Bill Penalty

Ravi, a lorry broker in Surat, managed 40–50 trips per month in Excel. His team generated e-way bills manually on the government portal. One busy Monday, a lorry left before the e-way bill was generated. GST inspector caught it at a check post — ₹10,000 penalty.

With Cargo Simplify: E-way bill is generated as part of the booking process — the lorry can't be dispatched without it.

Scenario 2: The Commission Dispute

Priya's transport company agreed to 2.5% broker commission on a large contract. Three months later, the fleet owner disputed one month's settlement, claiming the rate should have been 2%. Priya's Excel showed the rate but didn't have a clear audit trail of each booking.

With Cargo Simplify: Commission rate is locked at booking creation. Every booking shows the exact rate applied. No disputes — the system is the record.

Scenario 3: The 6-Month-Old Bilty Request

A CA firm asked their transport partner for all bilties from October 2025 for a GST audit. The transport manager spent 2 days going through old bilty books and Excel files, finding about 70% of them. 30% were physically lost.

With Cargo Simplify: Filter by date range → export all bilty PDFs in 2 minutes. Digital copies never get lost.

When is the Right Time to Switch?

You're ready to move from Excel to transport software when:

  • You're managing 10+ trips per month
  • You have 2+ staff members who need access to booking data
  • You've received at least one GST penalty or compliance notice
  • Month-end Tally entry takes more than 1 day
  • Commission disputes are a regular occurrence
  • You've lost or can't find old bilty copies
Rule of thumb: If your monthly freight turnover exceeds ₹5 lakh, the cost of compliance errors alone justifies switching to dedicated transport software.
